20-Nov-2026  Re-run after porting to Python 3  (see DONE.md)

Result 1 holds, result 2 does NOT and should not be relied on.  The 2012 code
let a card of any suit beat the card led (Card.__gt__ ignored suit unless
trump was involved) and Round.play_trick always had seat 0 lead whatever the
leader was, so it was not playing bridge.

With real trick rules (follow suit, only the lead suit or a trump can win),
fixed trump spades, "simpleHigh", 20000 deals per experiment, standard error
about 0.014 tricks:
1) rotating leader:      N-S 6.50, E-W 6.50 tricks  (matches 2012 result 1)
2) north always leads:   N-S 6.31, E-W 6.69 tricks  (the leading side wins
    slightly LESS: simpleHigh leads its highest card, often an ace that gets
    ruffed)
These are locked in, with looser bounds, by test_stats and
test_fixed_leader_is_not_an_advantage in Bridge/tests/test_round.py.
